A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Glad you asked.

What exactly is a relay?+

A relay is a workflow that starts with a trigger, uses AI to interpret context, and takes a series of actions. Think: a new enquiry arrives, its details are organized, a reply is drafted, and a teammate is notified.

Do I need to know how to code?+

The product is imagined around plain-language instructions. In this prototype, choose a starter workflow, run it with sample data, and review the result. There’s no code to write.

Can I approve things before they happen?+

Yes — human checkpoints are central to the concept. The interactive demo pauses before its final action so you can review and approve a sample. No real message is sent.

Can I connect my apps here?+

This is an independent product and website concept. The app connections, plans, and workflows show how Relay could work. No live integrations, payments, or AI services are connected.
